## Critical contract

At an exact opponent-owned Xerosic `1197` own-hand discard-to-three callback:

1. Compute exact-v3's action and require a full hand/option/serial bijection and exact discard count.
2. Require exactly one old, complete Bench Abra-line body `b`, no distinct ready backup, and exactly one parent-discarded card `m` that alone makes `b` pay its printed attack next own turn: Psychic Energy to unenergized Kadabra/Alakazam, Kadabra to energized old Abra, or Alakazam to energized old Kadabra.
3. If Active survives it must already attack or have a fully paid public retreat into `b`; if Active leaves, `b` must be a legal promotion. Do not predict opponent policy.
4. Require no other parent-retained one-card completer for `b`.
5. Require exactly one parent-retained victim `v` with zero guaranteed role in both branches. Duplicate capacity is counted per visible target; search capacity exists only if public copy accounting proves a target remains in deck. A possible live evolution, safe draw/search, legal attachment, recovery, retreat, attack, or present disruption target disqualifies a victim.
6. Swap only `m` for `v`, preserve the other two parent-retained serials, and bind the one-card completion through the next own turn. Any ambiguity, disruption, removal, unsafe fixed draw, or incomplete post-first-difference transaction clears/delegates and is a promotion defect.
