select or authorize an implementation.

- retain adjacent game 19;
- return to V2 on adjacent games 9, 18, 22, 25;

Reason to choose now: the current candidate has one genuine gain and a single,
narrow, source-verified selector defect. This option repairs known causal harm
without discarding the strongest new mechanism.

## Option B: deterministic public-effect registry expansion

Extend combat/return semantics for the high-priority effects in
`ROOT_PUBLIC_EFFECT_COVERAGE_GAPS.md`.

Start with exact deterministic effects:

- damage and HP modifiers;
- damage/effect prevention;
- attack locks;
- deterministic acceleration, switch, heal, recovery, and evolution access.

Reason to choose later: it reduces fail-closed silence and is necessary for
strong human play against the population. It does not by itself repair the
known doomed-evolution selector.

## Option C: own Trainer purpose and turn-order transactions

Replace generic item/supporter scores with complete card-purpose plans for
Pokégear, Explorer, Lillie, Night Stretcher, Cape, Ice Cream, Full Metal Lab,

## Root ordering recommendation for judgment

Root recommends evaluating A first, then B, then C. This is not an adoption
decision. The recommendation follows dependency order:

1. repair the known resource/selector defect;
2. expand what the combat/return layer understands;
3. use that layer to decide whether each Trainer and turn sequence has a
   concrete purpose.
