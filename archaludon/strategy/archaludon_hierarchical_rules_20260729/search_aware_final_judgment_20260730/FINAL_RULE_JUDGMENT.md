## Judgment

The intended mechanism is coherent and causally plausible. It replaces only
an exact-parent nonterminal Boss diversion with a public-count, high-confidence
attempt to finish through the unchanged opposing Active. Setup and ordinary
board formation remain parent-owned. On its certificate, it forms the Active
Archaludon, preserves immediate attacker readiness, commits the same turn
without an opponent-response interval, converts exactly three Prizes, and
finishes. It does not improve backup readiness; its two Alloy attachments are
terminal-line commitment rather than future-board value.

The source branch is deck-theory and public-information based. Runtime
eligibility contains no episode, row, seed, opponent identity, fixed fixture
serial, or facedown-card test. It derives the four-copy ledger from the frozen
deck and identified own public zones, requires complete public counts, and
binds current public serials semantically. Hidden identities occur only in the
test harness to instantiate hit and miss worlds.

The miss is a real cost, not a harmless rollback: probability `1/165`
(`0.6061%`) is paid after irreversible Ultra Ball use and discard of Cinderace
plus one Boss. The both-seat fallback proves legality and retained Boss access,
not outcome neutrality. This risk is proportionate only because the certified
hit is same-turn terminal and the displaced parent Boss is nonterminal. Every
natural miss or post-irreversible rollback therefore requires causal review.

The fixed matrix establishes adjacent, both-seat, duplicate, and inherited-floor
preservation only. It contains no trigger, no primary-anchor movement, and no
practical gain; none is credited. Absolute weakness against Kangaskhan/Crustle
(`28/80`) is inherited, not repaired. Formal-parent adoption is unsupported.

The fixed evidence also lacks a physical `panel` column and does not persist
candidate-internal rejection, caught-exception, rollback, or stale-transaction
telemetry. Exact trace equality proves no different emitted action in those
760 games, but cannot prove those internal counters were zero. This does not
invalidate entry into a fresh instrumented cumulative experiment; it may not
be cited as having closed those channels.
