# Continuity2 v1 — Phase B callback-safe execution

Phase B completes the narrow execution layer for the existing Continuity2
hypothesis:

- Duraludon successor readiness is based on its three-Energy Raging Hammer
  route; Hammer In is only an immediate fallback.
- Assemble Alloy binds one evolution card, visible discard Metal cards, and one
  stable lineage through `ACTIVATE`, exact `ATTACH_TO`, and every
  `ATTACH_FROM` callback.
- Turbo Flare chooses exactly 3/2/1/0 exposed deck Energy cards from the named
  primary deficit and fills one lineage before another.
- Manual attachments require a matching legal option. H0's final prerequisite
  can be forced; H1 development can only win within its existing action class.
- Retreat stores its exact public cost and target lineage, selects deterministic
  Energy payments, and promotes only the bound lineage. KO promotion is a fresh
  branch.
- Hero's Cape is planner-owned only when a legal Active attachment crosses a
  visible survival threshold.
- Night Stretcher selection and Ultra Ball protection activate only when the
  effect card and one named prerequisite are public.

The planner still fails closed for unsupported printed effects, does not assume
hidden deck Energy before Turbo's callback, does not reinterpret general
Pokegear/Boss choices, and performs no search, learning, or opponent-policy
prediction.
