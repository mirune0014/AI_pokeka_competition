## Caveats

- The fixed panel is directed, not an IID live sample. Two discordant gains
  give an imprecise effect estimate; this result proves retention and a valid
  mechanism, not a stable live rating.
- All nine fixed-panel activations occur on P1. Both seats are mechanically
  valid, but the panel supplies no positive P0 activation example.
- A strict Prize lead certifies the immediate exchange, not the whole reply
  sequence. It does not guarantee backup-attacker readiness, immunity to
  disruption, or survival of the promoted Alakazam.
- Several exact transactions are outcome-tied, including retained losses.
  The rule repairs a specific lost conversion lane; it is not a general answer
  to every Alakazam setup, targeting, or continuity failure.
- If new public episodes contain no v3 activation, a score movement must not
  be attributed to this rule.
