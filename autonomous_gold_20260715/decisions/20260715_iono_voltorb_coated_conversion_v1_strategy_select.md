# Strategy selection - Iono Voltorb Coated conversion v1

## Decision

The required read-only Sol-Ultra strategy judgment selected one isolated rule
hypothesis for implementation and fixed evaluation.  This is not an adoption,
package, or submission decision.

The two local Iono directories were root-hashed and found to be duplicate
copies:

- both `main.py` files SHA256
  `4DABA037B8F9A2FFA5B652604C821E2FBCE6B0148E1E7876D05540FFB336DE47`;
- both `deck.csv` files SHA256
  `BB264CCA591DF66A505B825C819557580606308DCA2CEE687C027164026BEA55`.

They are therefore one policy/deck population.  Only
`meta_agents/iono_bellibolt_live_wasabi_simple` may be counted.  The duplicate
name may not supply independent-policy evidence.

## Selected hypothesis

When Cinderace can pivot into an established three-Metal Duraludon and the
opposing Active Iono's Voltorb is immediately removable by Coated Attack,
prefer non-ex Archaludon only if the same-target ex would be publicly lethal to
Voltaic Chain or would expose the final two prizes without another one-prize
metal-line body.  The choice keeps the immediate knockout, keeps a one-prize
attacker, and adds Coated protection from the Basic Voltorb response.

The implementation boundary, exact public-state predicate, score, and
prohibited changes are frozen in the sibling evaluation
`IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC.md`.  There is no episode ID, step, turn, serial, fixed
Lightning count, or replay-history condition.

## Predeclared evidence standard

The single canonical Iono policy receives disjoint reference and fresh seed
panels, both seats, for 960 target keys.  It must expose at least 12 predicate
hits, at least six full Coated-protection chains, at least `+10/960`, zero
regressions, gains in both seats, and a paired normal-95% lower bound above
zero.  Historical Silver, Great Tusk/Crustle, Cubchoo, exact Lucario, and the
frozen broad panel must retain exact result/step identity.  Any failure retires
this exact implementation; thresholds and predicate may not be loosened.
