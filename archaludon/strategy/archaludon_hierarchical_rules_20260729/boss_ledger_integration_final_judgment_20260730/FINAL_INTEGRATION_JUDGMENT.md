## Rule-level reasoning

The hypothesis is coherent and remains exactly the selected one: when public
card conservation proves that three Boss copies are discarded and the fourth
is the uniquely held copy, prevent an exact-parent Ultra Ball discard from
consuming that last deterministic gust route only when one uniquely
plan-equivalent non-ex Archaludon can replace it while the same Metal discard
and complete current attack/backup continuation remain fixed.

That contract preserves setup and immediate board formation in the certified
branch; keeps the same ready attacker, Duraludon search, Bench development,
Energy disposition, attack, damage, and current Prize result; and retains a
finite Boss route to a publicly valuable Bench target. Terminal conversion,
forced defense, current Prize conversion, attack-completing Energy, current
survival, and lone-board formation all remain above it. Unfair Stamp and
unsupported public transitions erase deterministic access rather than
pretending hidden access survives.

The residual strategic risk is real: discarding non-ex Archaludon can remove a
later one-Prize attacker or evolution body, while the retained Boss may never
convert. Only one natural replay triggers the rule, and fixed-760 supplies no
primary-anchor, both-seat, repeated-bucket, adjacent-population, or finishing
gain. The inherited `35%` Kangaskhan/Crustle floor also remains. Therefore the
evidence is sufficient only for a contract-bound dormant integration, not for
formal adoption or a separate live strength claim.
