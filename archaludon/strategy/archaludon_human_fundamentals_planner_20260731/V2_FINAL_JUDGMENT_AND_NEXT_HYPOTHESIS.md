# V2 final judgment and next human-fundamentals hypothesis

## Rule-level verdict

**ACCEPT V2 as the safe parent/substrate for the next isolated
human-fundamentals implementation.**

This is substrate acceptance only. It is not a claim that V2 is stronger than
its parent, ready to package, or eligible for Kaggle.

The frozen-gate equality and the qualitative audit are sufficient for this
narrow decision:

- the candidate contains the complete byte-identical parent prefix;
- the fixed matrix preserved all 16 win/loss outcomes and every numerical
  floor;
- all eight first differences were classified from raw traces, with six
  intended one-Duraludon setup formations, two interchangeable-copy
  selections, and no harmful or unclassified first difference;
- the two known Explorer-preemption controls stayed parent-identical through
  Explorer and Poké Pad;
- focused, deterministic, legality, callback, duplicate, and engine checks
  passed without an action error or max-step hit.

This proves enough to reuse the architecture while keeping the exact parent as
the fallback. It does **not** prove statistical equivalence or strength:
candidate and parent were both `8/16`, all thresholds were met at their
boundaries, the conservative paired interval was `[-20.59 pp, +20.59 pp]`,
and Historical Silver seat 1 remained `0/4`.

An adjacent-population run is **not required before V2 is used as the source
parent for the next isolated candidate**. V2's exact-prefix fallback and the
fixed safety/first-difference evidence are adequate for that reversible
development choice. Adjacent-population confirmation is mandatory before a
descendant is adopted as the new practical parent or considered for packaging
or submission. The descendant evaluation below must include it.

## Exactly one selected hypothesis

### `PUBLIC_COMBAT_RETURN_DOMINANCE_V1`

**Hypothesis:** one symmetric, public-information combat/effect oracle, used by
one bounded complete-combat-plan layer with hard terminal and Pareto-dominance
arbitration, will correct basic attack, Active-evolution, combat-item, Tool,

3. Precedence is:
   engine/deck/reset/result and legality; exact inherited terminal or forced

### Hard decision gates

Compare the complete V2 plan and every supported candidate plan in this order:

1. **Exact current win:** select a legal plan that takes the final Prize this
   turn. Do not spend extra resources before the winning attack. If multiple
   exact wins are nondominated, keep V2.
2. **Exact next-turn loss avoidance:** when V2 permits a
   `READY_NOW/KNOWN_PUBLIC_RESOURCE` terminal reply, select a plan that removes
   every such terminal route only if it preserves this turn's attack and
   Prize result and does not create another equal terminal route.
3. **Public Pareto dominance:** otherwise a candidate must be no worse in all
   of:
   current win; current Prizes; exact current KO; exact hits to the same
   Prize; opponent certain return Prizes/terminal routes; current attacker
   survival or hits-to-KO; executable next attacker; next-turn payable attack;
   own exact turns to finish; opponent exact turns to finish; retained Metal,
   line pieces, Boss, recovery, and ACE SPEC; and exposure of one-/two-/three-
   Prize liabilities. It must be strictly better in at least one field.
4. Preserve attack continuity: if V2 attacks this turn, the candidate attacks
   this turn. A powered Bench Pokémon is not a backup unless promotion/switch
   and a payable attack are executable.
5. Full Metal Lab must be recomputed symmetrically. It cannot be played when
   saving the opposing Metal Pokémon loses a current KO/Prize or worsens the
   finishing clock.
6. Hero's Cape or Jumbo Ice Cream is consumed only when it changes a certified
   hit-to-KO, terminal route, or Prize clock. Otherwise reserve it.
7. The only permitted raw-damage decrease is Jumbo Ice Cream reducing Raging
   Hammer. That plan remains eligible only when current KO/Prize and exact
   hits-to-that-Prize are unchanged after all supported healing/return effects,
   while the heal removes a certain return KO and guarantees an additional
   attack or strictly shorter net Prize clock.
8. If all fields tie, any resource reserve worsens, or a hidden card is needed
   for the claimed benefit, return exact V2.

## Regression risks

- Wrong engine ordering for Weakness, Resistance, Lab, prevention, Sturdy, or
  damage-counter effects can reverse KO and survival proofs.
- Persistent-effect duration can leak across turns or be cleared too early.
- Cape HP transitions or Ice Cream healing can corrupt Raging Hammer math.
- A visible but unhandled Ability can make the opponent envelope falsely
  complete.
- Treating a possible manual attachment or hidden evolution as certain can
  waste resources; treating it as impossible can expose a loss.
- Evolution/Alloy/Turbo callbacks can collide with inherited transaction
  ownership or bind duplicate physical copies incorrectly.
- A bounded combat grammar can preempt an inherited information/disruption
  plan if comparability is not enforced.
- Broad activation raises regression surface; first-difference attribution,
  both seats, repeated seed blocks, and adjacent opponents are therefore
  mandatory.
